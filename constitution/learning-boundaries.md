# Learning Boundaries

What the Brain may learn automatically, and what it may not.

## May learn automatically (with logging, shadow-first)

- utility statistics for skills/tools/memories (counts, deltas);
- retrieval/activation metadata (meta-memory);
- prediction events, outcomes, prediction errors (raw records only);
- candidate policies, candidate skills, workflow engram candidates — as
  CANDIDATES in shadow stage;
- hindsight labels for bottleneck classification (training examples only).

## May NOT change automatically

- the Constitution;
- security boundaries, quarantine rules, permission rules;
- destructive-action policy (human approval boundaries);
- canonical semantic knowledge — only via curated reconsolidation with
  provenance and evidence;
- global policy promotion — only via the promotion lifecycle with evidence,
  baseline comparison, owner, version, and rollback (Phase 23 rules).

## Promotion lifecycle

```text
IDEA → SHADOW → MEASURED → ADVISORY → ACTIVE → MONITORED → REVISE / RETIRE
```

- Shadow mode changes no behavior; it logs `would_trigger`, `reason`,
  `expected_action`, `actual_outcome`, `hindsight_useful`.
- Every promotion to ACTIVE requires evidence, baseline comparison, rollback
  plan, owner, and version.
- Do not turn one model's quirk into a universal rule (model-scoped until
  cross-model evidence exists).

## Learning hardest from

surprise, corrections, verification failures, prediction errors.
