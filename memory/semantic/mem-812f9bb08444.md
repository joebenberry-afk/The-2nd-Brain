OMP 18.1.15 documented sessions/context: JSONL conversation trees support resume, continue, fork/branch, imports, exports and sharing. Compaction supports remote, snapcompact, handoff, shake and soft strategies with prerequisites; snapcompact is bounded image archival, not lossless retention. Consult versioned session.md and compaction.md before implementing transitions.
