- **DWC proportions win over fan-sprite proportions.** A new pass must never read worse at 1x than the previous one.
- **Multiple references per Digimon.** The owner's primary decides composition; the others fill canon details.
- **Palette:** retail palettes keep the rope pair B49C62+FFEE9C (179/216), used by the s16+ bound variant (not a back view).
- **Art agent:** DWC-Sprite-Artist at ~/.omp/agent/agents/. It can't spawn pixel-critic, so the parent runs the critic.
- **CM size:** the owner chose medium, ~0.65x of the fan sprite's native size.
