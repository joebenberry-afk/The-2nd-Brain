torch.compile on RTX 5070 Ti (sm_120), triton-windows 3.8.0.post29: compiled kernels are NOT bit-exact to eager even with emulate_precision_casts/division_rounding/disable_ftz, because ptxas (12.9 and 13.0) fuses LLVM's packed mul.rn.f32x2+add.rn.f32x2 into FMA despite --fmad=false. Fix: disable LLVM SLP vectorizer (patch triton.backends.nvidia.compiler.llvm.optimize_module disable_slp_vectorizer=True, TORCHINDUCTOR_COMPILE_THREADS=1); then EASU 1000/1000 bit-equal, no speed cost. See runs/bunet_v1/1b/speed/compile.md
