Windows: an out-of-context WinEvent hook (SetWinEventHook WINEVENT_OUTOFCONTEXT) wakes MsgWaitForMultipleObjectsEx(QS_ALLINPUT) on the hooking thread; verified by test with NotifyWinEvent from another thread. Use it instead of polling GetForegroundWindow (BU L4, fixes-1 b0624f6).
