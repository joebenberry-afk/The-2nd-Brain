BUFG research: Lee et al. CVPR2023 (arXiv 2202.07291) D-map paper says continuity 'cannot be defined with only two frames' but its own Table 2 shows 2-frame D-map variants recover ~half the GDM PSNR gain (AdaCoF-D(2) 30.097 vs (4) 30.212; CAIN-D(2) 30.616 vs (4) 31.011) and never addresses scene cuts; 4-frame layout is I0..I3 with target between I1,I2 so I3 = one extra holdback frame, but a causal 3-frame layout is untested. Don't cite it as proof that cut/HUD detection needs an extra held-back frame.
