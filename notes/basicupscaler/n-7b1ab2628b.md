Window-size game pairs can be fake: The Witcher 3 5.0 keeps its 3D render at the in-game Resolution and scales it to a smaller window (only UI redrawn), so bu pair gave a scaled render. Detect with the linear-fit check (best HR->LR least-squares filter from other pairs within 6 dB of the frozen still PSNR = scaled; Witcher 48.6 vs floor 43.7, real games 10-28 dB below) or PresentMon GPU busy not dropping at 720p. Now a per-game rule in BUNet QA (bunet-1a e7ffada).
