Research agents: WebFetch on many paper PDFs (cl.cam.ac.uk, arxiv.org/pdf, escholarship) returns 'binary content saved to ...tool-results/webfetch-*.pdf' instead of text, and big PDFs can hit the 300 s fetch deadline. Fix: run pdftotext (present at /mingw64/bin/pdftotext in Git Bash) on the saved file, then grep; plain mode keeps two-column reading order better than -layout.
