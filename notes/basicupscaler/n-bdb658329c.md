Outatime (MobiSys 2015) 4-degree 'imperceptible' navigation-error threshold is the authors' informal 'subjective assessment' (no method/N). Sec 4.4 says sub-4-degree errors (+2,-3,+3 deg) caused 'sufficiently noticeable' video shake needing a Kalman filter, and clients drop frames with excessive error even at RTT<=40ms. Threshold is camera-POSE error on engine-rendered frames (30 fps, 1024x1024, Doom3/Fable3), not RGB image-extrapolation error; do not use it to argue BUFG extrapolation at 8-17 ms is 'fine'.
