BUNet 1b speed (30 Sep 2026): triton-windows 3.8.0.post29 works on Python 3.14.7 with torch 2.14.0+cu130 (venv D:\venvs\bunet-speed). Exact torch.compile needs emulate_precision_casts, eager_numerics.division_rounding, eager_numerics.disable_ftz, numerics=strict AND the LLVM SLP vectorizer off (ptxas fuses mul/add.rn.f32x2 into FMA even with --fmad=false). Decision: compiled exact step + CUDA graphs + sync-free sampler, M=1; ~10 ms/member-step projected vs 41.9 eager. See runs/bunet_v1/1b/speed/decision.md
