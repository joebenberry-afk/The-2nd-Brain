Lesson (1 Oct 2026, BUNet B59): a numerical-equivalence canary (compiled vs eager gradients, per-tensor max|diff|/max|grad| <= 1e-5) passed at step 0 (~2e-7) but failed late in training (1.8e-5 to 3.4e-5) and passed again in another process (2.1e-6). cudnn.benchmark picks convolution algorithms by timing in each process, and late-training gradients are 50x smaller, so reorder noise crosses a fixed relative bar. Hold the reference's nondeterminism fixed (cudnn.deterministic, benchmark off) or scale the bar by eager-vs-eager noise.
