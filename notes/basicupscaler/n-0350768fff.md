Gotchas (collapse study, 1 Oct): (1) Run.state()['opt'] is the optimizer's live state_dict (tensors alias); an in-memory ring of states must deep-copy, and on CPU Optimizer.load_state_dict keeps the tensors it is handed, so restore from a copy. (2) Old checkpoints (smoke, cap_16x8_p0) predate the weights-only fix and runctl.load refuses them; use the exported .weights (net.load_weights) for analysis. (3) cap/data runs keep only the last two checkpoints: no pre-event state survives. (4) Restoring a net annealed to lr 5e-6 and stepping it at 4.85e-4 kills L7 within ~22 steps: never warm-restart at peak lr with this recipe.
