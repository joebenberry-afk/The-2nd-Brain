x264 writes H.264 VUI fixed_frame_rate_flag=0 even for constant-rate files (default b_vfr_input=1), so the flag cannot gate the VUI clock as a frame rate; cross-check time_scale/(2*num_units_in_tick) against the container rate instead (BU M3, fixes-1 2dad5f2).
