Meshy multi-view turnaround output order is NOT fixed. In one batch of 10, 0/1/2 came back as front/side/back for some sets and back/front/side or other orders for the rest. Always look at a contact sheet before naming the files `turnaround_v1_{0 back,1 front,2 side}`. The old jinwoo, milim and rimuru sets were front/side/back and were reordered on 2026-11-08. Milim's `battle_turnaround_v1_0` packs side and back views into one image.
