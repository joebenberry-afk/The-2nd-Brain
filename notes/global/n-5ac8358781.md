OMP bash here is a POSIX shell: PowerShell's call operator (`& "tool.exe" ...`) fails with `syntax error at line 1 col 1`, so drop the `&`; cmdlets like `Copy-Item` fail with `command not found` (use cp/ls/find, or `powershell -NoProfile -Command "..."`); cmd's `dir /s /b X` walks all of C:.
