Owner preference: use aria2 (aria2c) for every download, not curl/wget/Invoke-WebRequest.
Stated 2026-11-16 while a curl download of a release zip was running. Applies to anything fetched to disk: release archives, toolchains, models, datasets. Typical call: aria2c -x 16 -s 16 -k 1M -d <dir> -o <name> <url>. Small API/JSON reads into memory are not downloads.
