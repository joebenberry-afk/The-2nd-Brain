Deep-research gotcha: while writing a report from merged registers I cited claim numbers as source IDs (C095–C097 written as [S095–S097], C147 as S147). The independent evidence-auditor caught it. After drafting, always print the title of every cited S-ID and check it against the sentence it supports before delivery.
