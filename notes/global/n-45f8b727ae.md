OMP 18.11 floors its compaction trigger by its estimate of the stored conversation (pi-agent-core src/compaction/compaction.ts:357-374, compactionContextTokens = max(provider usage, stored estimate)).
So an extension `context` hook that shrinks requests (stubbing old tool output, Headroom-style) cuts per-request tokens and cost but never delays OMP compaction.
OMP also prunes old tool output itself and persists it (session-maintenance.ts #pruneToolOutputs; protect 40k, min saving 20k, cache-warm guarded). Its placeholders cannot be restored.
