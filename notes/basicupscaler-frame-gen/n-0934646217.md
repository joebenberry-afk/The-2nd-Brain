T13/PresentMon gotcha: if a PresentMon process is killed (or hangs before "Started recording") its ETW real-time session (named T13-<uuid> or the --session_name used) stays running with no consumer, and every later PresentMon capture then loses ~0.4-1M ETW events and its present records stop ~26 s in (11 Oct r3 cohort: 22 slots failed source coverage). Check `logman query -ets` for stale sessions and stop them with `logman stop <name> -ets` (works unelevated) before live measurements; parse "N ETW events were lost" in PresentMon's log as a quality failure.
