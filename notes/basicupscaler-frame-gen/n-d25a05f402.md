BU shader compilation (D3DCompile of all FG shaders) costs ~5 s per engine creation; it froze live FG on every game resize and delayed session start. Fixed in bufg-proof 3aab856 with a bytecode cache in gpu::compile_source (memory + %LOCALAPPDATA%\BasicUpscaler\shader-cache): 5.27 s cold -> 0.42 s warm. Also explains why each `bu fg` call takes ~5 s in offline suites (use the long-lived batch driver).
