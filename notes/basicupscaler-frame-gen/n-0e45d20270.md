Argon's SAHE report (research/reports/Hybrid Frame Generation Engine Architecture.md, 2026-10) is an in-engine design, not a capture design.
It stacks GFFE, Amulet, ExWarp/PatchEX, Disney's kernel-based interpolation and the FSR 3.1 swapchain behind a per-tile router. Every part BUFG can reach from capture already exists in the approved architecture: N-56, N-08, N-06, N-29, N-11 with AF-16 and AF-33, N-38, N-17, N-20, N-22, N-36 and the Instant/Balanced Response settings.
Assessment of 2026-10-13: do not pivot. Items worth harvesting: CGVQM (IntelLabs/cgvqm, Apache-2.0) as a candidate measure for T9 qualification, and a convex-hull, softmax-normalised kernel output constraint for N-25 and N-55.
Flaws found:
- Path C needs the next frame's colour.
- The FlowCast bound comes from flow-matching generative sampling, so it does not apply to scene motion.
- The AppSW latency claim is on BUFG's never-cite list (FG-201 C14).
- It has no cost model: GFFE alone takes 6.62 ms at 1080p (C060).
