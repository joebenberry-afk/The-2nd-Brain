PresentMon 2.6 opens its --output_file CSV exclusively while capturing (fopen_s-style sharing): any open() of the CSV during capture raises PermissionError(13). Never confirm capture start by reading the CSV; use metadata instead: `logman query <session_name> -ets` shows Status Running and a rising "Buffers Written", and os.stat(csv).st_size grows past the header within ~2 s (verified live 12 Oct). The T13 r5 cohort lost all 24 slots to this; mocked tests could not catch it, so live-smoke instrument changes before long cohorts.
