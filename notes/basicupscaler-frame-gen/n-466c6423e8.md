D30 cutover rule (lead, 14 Oct). When output size differs from capture size, the user's selected spatial upscaler stays the final resampler.
- Identity, or camera inactive: the path is unchanged and bit-exact.
- Active camera at 1:1 output: the standalone 9-tap Catmull-Rom re-pose replaces the final world draw into the screen target. This is the case FG-287 measured.
- Active camera with scaled output: compose the homography into a gather-based upscaler's source coordinates, keeping one resample. Otherwise re-pose at capture size into a free existing texture; report the VRAM cost before adding any new texture.
