FG-241 (10 Oct): 22:33 hard power-off did NOT reproduce on driver 617.42 at 1650/+300/+0 (53 W, margin ≥15 C) or 2400/+300/+500 (95 W, 83 C, T.Limit margin 0 within ~4 s). Cause most likely: NVIDIA driver install reloads the driver and drops G-Helper's nvidia-smi -lgc cap and offsets; G-Helper doesn't re-apply until restart, so the spike ran at stock. Fix: after any driver install, `schtasks /end` + `schtasks /run` the GHelper_S-1-5-21-... task (no admin needed; taskkill is denied), confirm "-lgc 0,<cap>" in %APPDATA%\GHelper\log.txt. bu-spike-d3d12.exe needs BU_SPIKE_GPU_LOCKED=1 when run outside tools.spike_d3d12.
