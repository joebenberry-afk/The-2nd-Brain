Antigravity hook contract (authoritative, from installed app bundles)

The current official Antigravity hook contract is embedded in Antigravity IDE bundles: out/main.js and out/jetskiAgent/main.js contain the exa/hooks_pb protobuf descriptor (base64 Lo("...")). JSON mapping is camelCase: common conversationId/workspacePaths/transcriptPath/artifactDirectoryPath; per-event args preInvocationHookArgs{invocationNum, initialNumSteps}, postInvocationHookArgs{...}, postToolHookArgs{stepIdx, toolCall{name,args}, error}, stopHookArgs{executionNum, terminationReason, error, fullyIdle}, preToolHookArgs{...}. Results: PreInvocation/PostInvocation {injectSteps:[{ephemeralMessage|userMessage|toolCall}]}, PostToolUse {}, Stop {decision, reason}, PreToolUse {decision, reason, overwrite, permissionOverrides}. Plugin hooks.json schema (from app UI): {"<group>": {"enabled": true, "PreInvocation": [{"matcher": "*", "hooks": [{"type": "command", "command": "...", "timeout": 30}]}]}} at ~/.gemini/config/plugins/<name>/hooks.json. The app UI also documents matcher values (e.g. "run_command|view_file").
