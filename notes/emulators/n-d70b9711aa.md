EmblemWeave `--build-engine` (N0 native build) fetches astc-encoder 5.7.0 tarball from GitHub after compiling modules; on this machine's slow link curl stalls (~18 KB/s) and resets after ~17 min, blocking the heavy lane.
Fix: don't gate emulator measurements on N0 build; retry fetch with `curl -L -C - --retry 10 --retry-all-errors`. Also: never `tail` EmblemWeave engine logs raw (curl progress = 1000+ lines); use `grep -v '^ *[0-9]* 33.65M'` or `tail -c 800`. `aria2c.exe` running under ~/.local/bin is the owner's own, not ours. Sub-agents on xai-oauth/grok-4.7 can die with ERR_TLS_CERT_ALTNAME_INVALID; revive via `write agent://<name>` with full remaining steps.
