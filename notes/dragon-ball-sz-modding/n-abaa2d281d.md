Joseph's stated goal (2026-08-10): AIplus should end up as **a whole new
difficulty setting**, not a transformation patch. The CPU it produces has to be
better at: transformations, movement, Ki usage, Blast Stock usage, melee,
combos, Blast attack usage, and strategic team switching.

**Why:** transformation is the first slice only because it is the cleanest to
isolate and measure, and because the level-20 curve tail made it a sharp
discriminator. Treating the transformation candidate as the deliverable
understates the target and leads to proposing a release when only one action
family has been touched.

**How to apply:** frame roadmap, candidate, and gate work as "which slice of the
Ultra+ tier is this" rather than "does the CPU transform more". Keep the
one-behavior-per-package rule for attribution, but sequence the slices toward
full coverage of the list above. Ultra+ overrides Ultra rather than adding a
sixth menu tier — `EKoratBattleCpuLevel` is a compiled native enum — so the
practical shape of the tier is "redefine what AI level 20 does". The measured
fact that makes this tractable: the level-20 keyframe is close to purely
subtractive, so the tier is largely about restoring level-19 competence and then
re-layering strategy personality. Related: [[telemetry-repo-ownership]],
[[aiplus-performance-constraint]].
