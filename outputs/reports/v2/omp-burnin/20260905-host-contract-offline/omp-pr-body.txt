## Problem
AsyncJobManager retains terminal status and agentId in its delivery snapshot and reconstructs them after live-row eviction. async-result details.jobs[] drops both fields. Extensions cannot safely recover them from rendered text or an expired live snapshot.

## Change
Six additive production lines: optional status?: AsyncJob["status"] and agentId?: string, populated exclusively from entry.job and forwarded to details.jobs[]. Rendering, retention, delivery ownership and existing schema payloads stay unchanged.
